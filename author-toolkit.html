The problem

Starting a pen name is a hundred decisions in a trench coat.

Genre, name, tropes, heat, series shape, covers, newsletter, launch plan — get them wrong and you find out six months and four books later. These tools front-load the decisions, pre-filled with what each genre's readers already expect, so you decide once, on purpose, and everything downstream inherits it.

FAQ

Do I need Claude (or any AI) to use these?

No — every tool works standalone and exports clean briefs you can use anywhere. But if you do use Claude, the tools are built for it: anything you leave blank exports as an instruction Claude fills in to fit the rest of your plan.

How are the tools delivered?

As links to your private toolkit pages (plus downloadable files). They run in any modern browser, save your work locally on your device, and work offline. Nothing to install, no account, no subscription.

I write romance AND thrillers — is this one purchase?

Yes. Both pen name builders are included. Downstream tools (First Three Books, Cover Brief, Reader Magnet, Launch Runway) work with either lane. Series Architect is a separate purchase.

Is my work private?

Completely. Everything saves in your own browser's storage — nothing is uploaded anywhere. Use the built-in export/backup buttons to keep copies.
