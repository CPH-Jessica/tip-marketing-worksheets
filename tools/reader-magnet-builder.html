🧲 Reader Magnet Builder

Plan the free story that turns readers into subscribers — the magnet, the funnel, and the welcome emails that make Book 1 inevitable.

1Whose list are we building?

A magnet works when it promises exactly what your books deliver. Import your Identity Kit so the magnet stays on-brand, or enter the lane by hand.

2Pick your magnet type

Each type has a job. Pick the one that matches where your catalog is right now.

3The magnet story

Small book, same promise. The magnet is many readers' first taste of your brand — it must deliver your signature tropes at your heat level, just shorter.

From your signature set — the magnet should lead with 1–2 of the same promises your books make.
The magnet's last page has one job: make Book 1 inevitable. What thread, character, or question hands the reader to your catalog?

4The funnel

A magnet without a funnel is just a free book. Wire the path: offer → signup → delivery → welcome → Book 1.

What the reader sees on the signup page / back-matter ad. Lead with the fantasy, not the format.
Email 1 delivers, Email 2 connects, Email 3 sells. Sketch each in a line — or leave for Claude.

5Your Magnet Plan

The whole funnel in one brief. Send the magnet itself to Story Studio as a project — it's a book too, just a small one.
