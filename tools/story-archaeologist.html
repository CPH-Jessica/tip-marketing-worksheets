🤝 The Story Promise

One sentence. Get it right and everything downstream — blurb, cover direction, reader trust — falls into place. If it won't fit in one sentence, the story isn't focused yet, and it's a thousand times cheaper to find that out here than in chapter fourteen.

The three rules: ① ONE sentence — if it won't fit, the story is unfocused. ② Specific, not generic — "falls in love" is nothing; "forced to share a safe house during a vampire council hunt" is a promise. ③ The hiding beat is critical — that's your middle 200 pages.
