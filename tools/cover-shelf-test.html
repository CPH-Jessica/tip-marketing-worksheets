🔍 Cover Shelf Test

Drop in your cover and see it the way a reader does: postage-stamp small, in a grid of competitors, in one second. Score it honestly, then take the critique to Claude.
🔒 Everything runs in your browser. Your cover and your competitor screenshots never leave this device — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

1Drop in your cover

JPG or PNG, front cover only. The ebook file you would upload to KDP is the right one to test.

🖼️
Drag your cover here, click to choose a file, or paste it from your clipboard.

Specs

📥 Found your Cover Brief on this device.

2The thumbnail wall

These are the sizes readers actually meet your cover at. Store layouts shift, so treat the pixel counts as close enough, not gospel.

The Kindle app and many phones show the store on a dark background.
Half-close your eyes. At the two smallest sizes: can you read the title? Could a stranger name the genre? If not, nothing else on this page will save it — fix that first.

3The shelf

Readers never see your cover alone. Drop in the covers it will sit beside — screenshot the top twelve in your subcategory — and see whether yours belongs there and still gets the click.

Drop competitor covers here (several at once is fine) or click to choose. They stay on this device.

4Squint, grayscale, e-ink

Blur strips the detail and leaves the shapes; grayscale strips the color and leaves the contrast. If the focal point and the title survive both, the cover will survive a thumbnail.

5What the pixels say

Counted from the image itself: contrast, clutter, color, and whether one thing dominates. Numbers, not taste — this cannot tell you if the cover is beautiful or on-genre. That is step 6.

6The reader checklist

Now you score it. Be the stranger scrolling at 11 p.m. with a thumb on the screen, not the author who knows what the symbols mean. 1 = no, 5 = absolutely.

Paste the prompt into Claude and attach the cover image — Claude cannot see the picture from the words alone. Drag the shelf image in too if you want the comp check grounded in what you actually saw.
