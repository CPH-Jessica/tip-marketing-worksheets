▸ How to find ASINs on Amazon
  1. Go to Amazon and search for books similar to yours (same genre, tropes, audience).
  2. Click on a book's product page.
  3. Scroll down to "Product details".
  4. Find "ASIN" — it's a 10-character code starting with B (e.g., B0DXXXXXX).
  5. Alternatively, look at the URL — the ASIN is often in the path: amazon.com/dp/B0DXXXXXX
  6. Pro tip: Target books with 50-500 reviews. They have enough traffic to matter but aren't so massive you'll be outbid.
Strategy note: Product targeting puts your ad on competitors' book pages. This works best when your book has a strong cover and good reviews — you're asking readers to choose YOU instead.

Campaign 3 — Auto Campaign

Loading campaign name...
How Auto Campaigns Work: Amazon's algorithm chooses where to show your ad based on your book's metadata — title, subtitle, description, categories, and keywords. You don't pick any targets.
Why run an Auto campaign?
  • Discovery: Amazon will find keywords and ASINs you never thought of.
  • Data mining: After 2-4 weeks, check your Search Term Report. The converting terms become targets for your manual campaigns.
  • Low risk: With a lower budget and bid, you're spending less while Amazon does the research for you.
  • Always-on safety net: Many authors run auto campaigns indefinitely alongside manual ones.
