3. Prompt builder — three principles, then assemble

1. Specificity beats vibesSubject + composition + setting + light. “A romance cover” is slop. An overhead flat-lay with named colors and a light direction is usable.
2. Exclude what you do not wantModels add text, faces, and extra hands by default. Tell them no. Every prompt below ends in exclusions.
3. Style descriptor last“Painterly digital illustration.” “Editorial photography, film grain.” This is the lock. Put it at the end.

Homework — after you generate and add type

4. Eight-point thumbnail test

Shrink the finished cover (type on it) until it is the size of a postage stamp. Then check these. One fail is a revision, not a vibe issue.

Last ten minutes of class

5. Legal gate — 60 seconds of not winging this

A prompt-only AI image has no human authorship and cannot be copyrighted. Treat the model output like a stock image you have a license to use. Typography, crop, composite, color-grade — that is your design work. Document it. Finishing in Canva does not let you skip KDP’s AI-generated checkbox if the underlying image came from a model. Disclosure is confidential. Failure to disclose is what gets books pulled. Do not prompt living artists by name. Do not prompt real people.
