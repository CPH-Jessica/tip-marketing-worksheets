Your Standard Membership

Every workshop on this page is free to attend live with your Standard membership. No upgrade, no extra fee — show up, bring a book idea, and leave with something built.

These aren't watch-and-take-notes sessions. We open the same worksheets together and you fill yours in while I fill mine in. Come with a real book in mind, even a fuzzy one.

What your Standard membership gets you

  • Live attendance at every workshop — all of them, always free to you.
  • The worksheets during the session — I drop the links in chat and we work through them together, live.
  • Q&A in the room — ask me anything about your own book while we're in it.
  • The community thread afterwards — where people post what they built and keep each other honest.
