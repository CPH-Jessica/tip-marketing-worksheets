Beta Reader Recruitment Email Copied!

Subject: Would you beta read my upcoming [GENRE] novel? Hi [NAME], I'm looking for a small group of thoughtful readers to beta read my upcoming novel, [BOOK TITLE]. It's a [GENRE] story about [one-sentence hook]. What I need: - Read the full manuscript within 3 weeks - Answer a short feedback questionnaire (10 questions) - Be honest — I want real reactions, not just praise What you get: - A free early copy of the book - Your name in the acknowledgments (if you'd like) - My eternal gratitude The manuscript will be ready by [DATE]. Interested? Just reply and I'll add you to the list. Thanks so much, [YOUR NAME]

ARC Reader Invitation Email Copied!

Subject: Get a FREE early copy of [BOOK TITLE] — ARC team invite! Hi [NAME], I'd love to invite you to my ARC (Advance Reader Copy) team for my new [GENRE] release, [BOOK TITLE], launching on [RELEASE DATE]. Here's how it works: - You receive a free digital copy 3 weeks before release - You read and post an honest review on Amazon and/or Goodreads on or around release day - No pressure to leave a positive review — honest opinions only The book is about [one-sentence hook]. If you're in, click the link below to sign up: [SIGNUP LINK] Thank you for supporting indie authors! [YOUR NAME]

Gentle Review Reminder (Post-Launch) Copied!

Subject: Quick reminder about [BOOK TITLE] 💛 Hi [NAME], I hope you've had a chance to read [BOOK TITLE]! If you enjoyed it (or even if it wasn't your cup of tea), I'd truly appreciate a short review on Amazon or Goodreads. Even 1-2 sentences makes a huge difference for indie authors. Here are the links: - Amazon: [LINK] - Goodreads: [LINK] Not sure what to say? Here are some prompts: - What did you enjoy most? - Who would you recommend this book to? - How did it make you feel? No worries if life got busy — I completely understand. Thank you either way! Warmly, [YOUR NAME]

Thank You Email for Reviewers Copied!

Subject: THANK YOU for reviewing [BOOK TITLE]! Hi [NAME], I just saw your review of [BOOK TITLE] and wanted to say a heartfelt THANK YOU. Your support means the world to me as an indie author. Reviews are the single best thing readers can do to help a book succeed, and I'm so grateful you took the time. As a thank-you, I'd love to [offer something — e.g., send you my next ARC first, give you a bonus epilogue, etc.]. Thank you again, [YOUR NAME]
