## Full product description

**These are real notes you'd otherwise pay an editor to flag.**

You finished the draft. Now comes the part where most indie books quietly fall apart: the edit. One "read it again and fix stuff" pass will never catch what nine targeted passes will — and hiring it all out costs more than most books earn back.

The **Story Clinic** is the capstone editing cockpit from Edit Like a Boss — your whole self-editing system on one screen:

- **Instant offline scans** against the AI banned-word list — the words and tics that make prose smell machine-written, flagged before a single prompt is run
- **Ready-to-paste prompts for all 12 editing passes** — Quality Check, Plot Hole Checker, Timeline Consistency, Line Edit, Dialogue Polish, Heat Scene Edit, Developmental Edit, Voice Audit, Engagement/Hook Audit, Arc Pacing Audit, Present-Moment Pass, and the Structural De-AI Sweep
- **Edit or Review, your choice** — chapter passes come in two flavors: ✏️ Edit (fix + change log) or Review (notes only). The audit passes are review-only, because those fixes are story decisions that belong to you
- **Five story lab reports** — plot, character, pacing, conflict, timeline. Whole-book analysis, the kind bestseller-analysis dashboards charge monthly for
- **Reader-satisfaction tracker** — see the emotional charge curve of your book, chapter by chapter
- **One exportable Manuscript Report** — before & after edits, every note in one document

**Works with the AI you already use.** Every prompt is calibrated for Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or Grok — no API key, no subscription to us, no new tool to learn. And it edits in **six languages**: English, French, German, Italian, Spanish, and Portuguese.

**Private by design.** The Story Clinic is a single HTML file that runs entirely in your browser. Your manuscript is never uploaded anywhere. Paste a chapter or the whole book — it stays on your machine.

**Who it's for:** Indie authors with a finished draft who want an editor-grade checkup without the editor-grade invoice — and without feeding their book to a random website.
