## Full product description

**Every subgenre is a promise to a specific reader. Do you know exactly what yours is promising?**

Most new pen names fail before book one is even written — not because the writing is bad, but because the brand is fuzzy. The covers say one thing, the blurb says another, and the reader who *would* have devoured the whole series never clicks.

The **Pen Name Identity Builder** fixes that in one sitting.

**Pick your lane. Get the baseline. Make it yours.**

It's a guided, 4-step interactive tool:

**Step 1 — Pick your lane.** Choose from **26 romance subgenre baselines** across 7 groups: Steamy Contemporary, Sweet & Closed Door, Dark & Dangerous, Historical, Paranormal & Romantasy, Mystery & Suspense, and Short Reads & Rapid Release.

**Step 2 — Your baseline, prebuilt.** Every lane auto-fills the 8 things readers expect before they'll one-click:

- The reader promise (what your subgenre swears to deliver)
- Core tropes readers came for
- Pen-name vibe — with example names
- Brand look: covers & colors
- Voice & tone
- Comp authors to study
- Length & release rhythm for the lane
- Watch-outs & conventions (the mistakes that get you one-star reviews)

Plus a heat-level default calibrated to your lane.

**Step 3 — Make it yours.** Tune the heat, toggle your signature tropes, pick your three brand words, write your tagline, sketch your reader avatar, draft your author bio, set your release plan and boundaries. You don't have to reinvent the lane — you have to deliver it in your voice.

**Step 4 — Your Pen Name Identity Kit.** One clean, exportable brief: copy it, download it as markdown, or grab the ready-made prompt and hand it straight to Claude (or ChatGPT) to keep building.

**What you get:**

- One self-contained HTML file — open it in any browser, forever
- No login, no subscription, no software to install
- Autosaves your work in your browser as you go
- Nothing is uploaded anywhere — your pen name plans stay yours
- Your finished Identity Kit exports as markdown and plugs directly into series-planning tools

**Who it's for:** Romance authors launching a new pen name — or rebranding one that never quite found its readers — who want to nail genre expectations *before* writing 80,000 words into the void.
