## Creative notes

- **Format:** 1:1 (1080×1080) for feed + 9:16 (1080×1920) for Stories/Reels. Static images outperform for tool launches; add one simple screen-recording video ad later if statics get traction.
- **Pen Name Builder visuals:** (a) screenshot of the 26-lane picker over the rose-gold/plum brand palette, (b) the exported Identity Kit as a "document" mockup, (c) a split image — "fuzzy brand" vs "one clear promise". Keep text overlay under ~20% of the image.
- **Story Clinic visuals:** the cockpit tabs, or a "clinic clipboard" motif with the 12 passes listed like a chart. Lean on the medical-checkup metaphor — it's distinctive.
- **On-image text that works:** "26 romance lanes. One pen name." / "$47 launch sale" / "Bring your manuscript in for its checkup."

## Targeting notes

- **Audiences (cold):** interests — Self-publishing, Kindle Direct Publishing, Amazon KDP, romance novels + writing, NaNoWriMo, The Creative Penn, 20BooksTo50k/author conferences. Layer: engaged with page OR interest stacks; women 25–65 for romance campaign; broader 22–65 for genre fiction ad.
- **Warm:** email list upload, page/IG engagers 365d, site visitors (add the Meta pixel to Payhip — Payhip supports it in Settings → Marketing).
- **Budget shape:** start $10–20/day on Campaign 1 with ads 1–3, let Meta pick the winner for 3–4 days, kill losers, then add ads 4–5. Story Clinic pre-order runs best to WARM audiences only until the deliverable ships.
- **Landing pages:** send romance ads to the Pen Name Identity Builder listing, ad 5 to the Fiction edition listing/variant. Never send cold traffic to a pre-order without the delivery date visible above the fold.
