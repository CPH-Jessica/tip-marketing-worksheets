Which floor are you on?

Read the statements. The floor where you catch yourself nodding is the one built for you.

🏛️ You're in the Lobby if...

  • I'm newer to publishing, or starting a fresh pen name, and everything feels like it's supposed to be done at once.
  • People keep saying newsletter, reader magnet, Claude Project, and I don't actually have any of them set up.
  • I don't need to go faster. I need to know I'm going in the right direction.

That's the ground floor. Start in the Lobby.

🌿 You're in the Conservatory if...

  • I have books, a magnet, a BookFunnel account, a MailerLite login. None of it talks to each other.
  • I can't tell which part is actually broken. I just know the numbers aren't moving.
  • My AI drafts still sound like AI, and my newsletter signup count makes me want to close the tab.

That's the middle floor. Grow in the Conservatory.

🔥 You're in the Penthouse if...

  • I run multiple pen names with real backlists, and my problem is scale, not setup.
  • I want automation, architecture, and honest talk about what AI can and can't do yet.
  • I'm done with classrooms. I want a peer room that keeps up with me.

That's the top floor. Take the Penthouse.

Questions authors actually ask

What if I can't attend live?

You still get the whole month. The replay is posted within 24 hours, the tools drop arrives either way, and the tier classroom in Skool is open all month for questions. The live room is the best version, but it is not the product. The system is the product.

Can I switch floors?

Yes. Billing is monthly, so the natural moment to change floors is when a new month starts. If you're mid-month and certain you're on the wrong floor, email coach@theinvisiblepen.com and we'll sort it out like adults.

Do I need a Skool membership to join?

Your tier's private classroom, the replays, and the between-session conversation live inside the Skool community, so yes, you'll want to be a member there. Cohort fees are in addition to Skool membership. VIP members ($499/month) already have a cohort seat included.

What happens if my floor fills up?

The caps are real: 25, 20, and 12. When a floor is full, booking closes. Email coach@theinvisiblepen.com to get on the waitlist and you'll be first to hear when a seat opens.

How do I cancel?

Billing is monthly and you can cancel anytime. No blocks, no long commitment, no exit interview. You keep everything that was dropped while you were a member.

When do sessions happen?

The cohorts start in September 2026. Each floor meets once a month for 90 minutes on Zoom. Exact days and times are announced at the AI Talk and inside Skool, and they're listed on the booking page when you grab your seat.
