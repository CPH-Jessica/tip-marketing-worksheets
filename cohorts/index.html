Which cohort fits you right now?

Read the statements. The cohort where you catch yourself nodding is the one built for you.

🧱 You're a Building Author if...

  • I'm newer to publishing, or starting a fresh pen name, and everything feels like it's supposed to be done at once.
  • People keep saying newsletter, reader magnet, Claude Project, and I don't actually have any of them set up.
  • I don't need to go faster. I need to know I'm going in the right direction.

That's your room. Start with Building Authors.

🌿 You're a Growing Author if...

  • My book is out — or up for pre-order — and I have a magnet, a BookFunnel account, a MailerLite login. None of it talks to each other.
  • I can't tell which part is actually broken. I just know the numbers aren't moving.
  • My AI drafts still sound like AI, and my newsletter signup count makes me want to close the tab.

That's your room. Join Growing Authors.

🚀 You're a Scaling Author if...

  • I have ten or more books out — often across pen names — and my problem is scale, not setup.
  • I want automation, architecture, and honest talk about what AI can and can't do yet.
  • I'm done with classrooms. I want a peer room that keeps up with me.

That's your room. Step up to Scaling Authors.

Questions authors actually ask

What if I can't attend live?

You still get the whole month. The replay is posted within 24 hours, the tools drop arrives either way, and your tier's private classroom is open all month for questions. The live room is the best version, but it is not the product. The system is the product.

Can I switch cohorts?

Yes. Billing is monthly, so the natural moment to change cohorts is when a new month starts. If you're mid-month and certain you're in the wrong room, email coach@theinvisiblepen.com and we'll sort it out like adults.

Do I need a Skool membership to join?

No. The cohorts are open to everyone. Whether you found us through Skool, the Substack, or Facebook, you just sign up, book your seat, and you're in. After you enroll, you get access to your tier's private classroom for between-session questions. Skool VIP members ($499/month) already have a cohort seat included.

What happens if my cohort fills up?

The caps are real: 25, 20, and 12. When a cohort is full, booking closes. Email coach@theinvisiblepen.com to get on the waitlist and you'll be first to hear when a seat opens.

How do I cancel?

Billing is monthly and you can cancel anytime. No blocks, no long commitment, no exit interview. You keep everything that was dropped while you were a member.

When do sessions happen?

The cohorts start in September 2026. Each cohort meets once a month for 90 minutes on Zoom. Exact days and times are announced at the AI Talk, on the Substack, on Facebook, and in Skool, and they're listed on the booking page when you grab your seat.
