Manuscript Editing Cockpit

Welcome to your Editing Cockpit

This is the capstone companion to Edit Like a Boss. It runs the whole editing system on one screen: instant offline checks against the AI banned-word list, ready-to-paste prompts for all 12 reasoning passes, a reader-satisfaction tracker, and one exportable fix-it report with before & after edits.

★ Best way to use it

Open this tool inside the AI project that already holds your book files (your manuscript, style sheet, character codex, pen-name voice). When the prompts live next to your story, your AI has the full context and the notes come back sharper.

✓ Works standalone too

No project? No problem. Paste a chapter (or the whole book) right here and go. Everything stays in your browser — nothing is uploaded, no account, no API key.

⤳ Any AI tool

Every prompt is copy-paste and tool-agnostic — drop it into Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or Grok. Bring the notes back here and export your report.

New to this? Work one chapter at a time — paste a chapter, run the passes, then use + Add next chapter below to build your book up as you go. That matches the course, lesson by lesson.

The Charge Model — why tension alone undersells the book

Tension is potential energy: charge stored at the boundary between two people who want and can't yet have. When they finally fall into each other, that energy isn't lost — it converts into Bond, the closeness that now flows between them. Reader satisfaction tracks the whole system: bond earned, tension still live, stakes that make it matter, and the bright catharsis of release.

The move to watch: at a good payoff, Tension drops but Satisfaction spikes and settles at a higher floor than before, because Bond rose to catch it. The failure it flags is a leak — tension fell but bond didn't rise to meet it, so the charge escaped instead of passing between them.
