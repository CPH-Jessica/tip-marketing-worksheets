The Charge Model — why tension alone undersells the book

Tension is potential energy: charge stored at the boundary between two people who want and can't yet have. When they finally fall into each other, that energy isn't lost — it converts into Bond, the closeness that now flows between them. Reader satisfaction tracks the whole system: bond earned, tension still live, stakes that make it matter, and the bright catharsis of release.

The move to watch: at a good payoff, Tension drops but Satisfaction spikes and settles at a higher floor than before, because Bond rose to catch it. The failure it flags is a leak — tension fell but bond didn't rise to meet it, so the charge escaped instead of passing between them.
