3. Choose Your Stack Pattern

Click the card that fits your situation. You can change this later. Most authors land in Pattern B.

AThe Minimalist

Claude only
Solo authors who don't want to manage multiple tools. All 8 passes in one place.
Pros Simplest workflow. Voice preservation across every pass. One login. Cons No NotebookLM cross-referencing. No GPT knowledge files. No image gen.
Recommended

BThe Two-Tool Stack

Claude + Gemini (NotebookLM)
Most authors' sweet spot. Claude for editing. NotebookLM for plot/timeline. Gemini Image for visuals.
Pros Best voice + cross-referencing + image gen. Covers what most authors need. Cons Two paid subscriptions. Mild overhead.

CThe Full Stack

Claude + Gemini + ChatGPT (+ Grok)
High-volume authors. Multiple pen names. Multiple books in production at once.
Pros Maximum flexibility. Redundancy across platforms. Cons ~$80-120/mo. Cognitive overhead. Time to maintain across four tools.
