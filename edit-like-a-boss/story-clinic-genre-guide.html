1

Import your manuscript

  • On the home screen, click ⬆ Import your manuscript. Type your Book title, then either Choose a Word or text file (.docx, .txt or .md — or drop it on the box) or paste into Or paste your manuscript.
  • Chapters are detected from headings like Chapter 1, Chapter One, # Chapter 1, Prologue or Epilogue. The counter under the box tells you how many it found before you commit — if it says one chapter and you know there are thirty, fix the headings first.
  • Loading a single chapter with no heading? Type its name in Section / chapter title. Then hit Add to manuscript →.
  • Working chapter by chapter? Use ⬆ Import into this book (top bar, or the ＋ row under your chapter list) — new chapters land at the end and everything you've already decided is kept.
  • Several books? ＋ New book starts another; the home screen lists them all under Pick up where you left off.
💡 Try Explore a sample first — it's a cozy mystery (The House on Alder Street) deliberately riddled with problems, so you can watch every feature fire before trusting it with your baby.
2

Set your document & voice settings

Click ⚙ Document & voice settings at the bottom of the sidebar. Everything here is folded into every prompt the clinic builds.

  • Document title and Type / genre (e.g. locked-room mystery, grimdark fantasy, near-future thriller).
  • Voice lane — pick the one closest to your book: epic fantasy, urban fantasy, science fiction, space opera, horror, psychological thriller, cozy mystery, or Custom / my own voice.
  • Intensity level, honestly: 1 cozy (violence off-page) → 5 grimdark (nothing softened). Every pass holds your book to it — a cozy never shows what a grimdark would.
  • Manuscript language — English, French, German, Italian, Spanish or Brazilian Portuguese. Every prompt asks your AI to analyze and reply in that language (the instant Auto-Scan stays English-tuned). The same dropdown sits on the pass card, so you can switch without opening settings.
  • Create your voice profile — click ⧉ Copy voice extraction prompt, paste it into the AI project that has your voice skill (or give it two or three approved samples), then paste what comes back into Your reviewed voice profile, voice notes, or writing sample. Fix anything that doesn't sound like you — this box outranks the lane description.
  • Author rules & protected choices — invented spellings, a term that must never be "corrected", the deliberately unreliable narrator. Rules beat generic preferences.
  • Document context, outline & continuity notes — world bible facts, the magic or tech rules, what has to stay true from earlier books. The World & Rules audit leans on this.

Click Done. Settings save with the book and auto-save as you type.

3

Run a chapter sweep

Pick a chapter under Manuscript (or click Editing passes in the sidebar). The pass card at the top of the workspace has two dropdowns side by side:

  • The pass dropdown — grouped into Chapter sweeps (1–9) and Whole-book audits. A pass you've already reviewed on this chapter shows ✓ in the list; one you reviewed before the chapter changed shows ↻. The line above it reads Pass N of 9 · 3 of 9 reviewed on this chapter, and a one-line brief of the pass sits underneath.
  • The Manuscript language dropdown — English, French, German, Italian, Spanish or Brazilian Portuguese. Change it and every prompt asks your AI to analyze and reply in that language; a replies in French pill appears on the card so you can't forget it's set.

Choose your pass, then click ✦ Start this pass → to open the Work through this pass dialog.

Two ways to run it

  • Copy / paste into my own AI (the default): first choose the answer format under What did your AI return? — the prompt changes with that choice, so pick it before you copy. Then ⧉ Copy prompt, paste it into Claude/ChatGPT/Gemini/Grok, and bring the reply back.
  • Built-in AI: open ✦ AI connections (sidebar) or ⚙ AI setup (top bar), paste your own OpenAI or Claude API key, click Test & save changes, pick a model. Back in the pass dialog, choose that provider in the Built-in AI dropdown → Prepare request (shows a token estimate) → Run with AI. The reply drops straight into the box and the review opens itself. Your provider bills you directly — set a spending limit with them first. Keys are saved only in your browser.

The three answer formats

  • Suggestions (JSON, including code blocks) — recommended. Your AI returns a list of exact before → after edits with a reason for each. Short reply, one decision card per edit. This is the one to use almost always.
  • Revised chapter — the whole text, rewritten. Your AI edits the chapter and hands the full text back (in parts if it's long — say "NEXT" until you have them all, combine, then paste). The clinic compares it to your chapter paragraph by paragraph and turns every difference into a decision card.
  • Notes / report — nothing to apply. Review-only notes. Saved to the pass and folded into your Manuscript report; they never rewrite your text.

Paste the reply under 2 · Paste the response and click Review proposed changes → (or Save notes to this pass → for notes).

💡 The nine sweeps, in the order the class teaches them: 1 Structural De-AI Sweep · 2 Present-Moment Pass · 3 Quality Check · 4 Engagement / Hook Audit · 5 Developmental Edit · 6 Action & Set-Piece Edit (choreography, physical consequences, clarity and intensity — the fight, the chase, the breach) · 7 Voice Audit · 8 Dialogue Polish · 9 Line Edit. Finished a pass in your head with nothing to change? I reviewed this pass — no changes in the decisions panel ticks it off.
4

Review the changes — you have the final say

The right-hand panel becomes Your editing decisions with a counter of how many are left. Each card shows Original, Suggested, and the editor's reason.

  • Show passage in manuscript highlights that exact spot in the reader so you can see it in context.
  • ✓ Accept takes the suggestion. Keep original leaves your words alone. Click a chosen button again to un-choose it.
  • Edit it myself / discuss opens Make this edit your own: type your version and Accept my wording, or describe what you want ("keep the dread, lose the adverbs") and ⧉ Copy discussion prompt to get two or three alternatives from your AI.
  • Accept all / Keep all mine decide the whole list in one click; Cancel throws the review away untouched.
  • Finish review writes only the accepted changes into the running manuscript. Anything undecided stays as written. A toast confirms how many landed, with an Undo button for a few seconds.

What the marks mean

  • In the pass dropdown that pass now carries a ✓ — reviewed on this version of the chapter — and the card's count ticks up (4 of 9 reviewed on this chapter). The panel shows Pass reviewed with "kept 7 of 12 changes", plus 👀 Tracked changes (removals struck through, additions marked, computed on your computer).
  • Edit the chapter again and the mark becomes ↻ Chapter changed since — the pass still counts, but Run again is there whenever you want a fresh read. In the sidebar the chapter shows its running count (3/9) until all nine sweeps are ✓, then the chapter itself gets a ✓.
  • The next pass you run is built from the new version automatically. Dev edit → review → action pass → review. Your changes carry forward the whole way.
💡 Reader tabs: Current chapter is the running text (click Edit text to type changes by hand, then Save text), Full manuscript shows the whole book with your accepted edits, Original is the untouched import — Restore this original puts it back if you ever want a do-over.
5

Run the whole-book audits

The Whole-book audits group at the bottom of the pass dropdown holds five passes. Pick one and the card's label changes to Whole-book audit: they read every chapter at once (the reader flips to Full manuscript) and they are review-only on purpose — they flag; the story decisions are yours.

  • Plot Hole Checker, Timeline Consistency (elapsed time, travel, who knew what when) and Arc Pacing Audit.
  • World & Rules Consistency — your magic system, tech and world rules obey their own laws, tracked in a World Bible log (rule, where it's established, where it's used or broken).
  • Clue & Fair-Play Audit — every clue the sleuth uses was on the page for the reader too, with a clue ledger and a suspect grid (means, motive, opportunity, alibi).
  • Click ✦ Start this pass → (or Open audit in the panel). Copy the prompt or run it with your key exactly as in step 3 — there's no format to choose, an audit always comes back as a report.
  • Paste the report and click Save audit report →. It appears in the Audit notes panel with Copy report and Run again, and it's folded into your Manuscript report.
  • Go back to the chapters the audit points at and fix them with the sweeps. If the manuscript changes after an audit, the panel says manuscript changed since and the audit's mark in the dropdown turns ↻ — rerun it when you're ready.
6

Check your Story lab

Four lab views live under Story lab in the sidebar. All the instant reads are computed in your browser — nothing uploads.

  • ⚡ Auto-Scan — fires the moment chapters load and flags banned words, AI-isms, dramatic triplets, flash-forwards ("she would later realize…"), filter words and weak verbs. Red (critical) first, always — those are the "AI wrote this" tells; the sidebar badge counts them. Show highlighted text lights up every hit inside your prose; Open this chapter → jumps you to it with the first sweep ready.
  • 📈 Story reports — seven whole-book reports: 📈 Plot (your emotional arc — ~90% of bestsellers reach both tinted bands), 🎭 Character (cast and world glossary: presence bars, confusable names, who vanishes, dialogue share), ⏱ Pacing (watch the middle third), ⚔️ Conflict (chapters that have gone quiet), 🕰 Timeline (floating chapters, time skips, impossible travel math), 🕯 Dread (horror and thriller writers, this one's yours: the quiet-quiet-BANG wave, escalation across the book, and numbing) and 🔍 Mystery (the investigation engine: is the inciting crime in by ~15%, do reveals keep landing, does the answer avalanche in all at once at the end?). Each has a deep-dive prompt for your AI and a paste-back box that feeds the Manuscript report.
  • ♥ Satisfaction tracker — the Charge Model from class, tuned for genre: tension released should convert into reader investment; a payoff is a reveal, a victory, a truth finally spoken. 1 · Copy "rate my beats" prompt → let your AI score the book → 2 · Paste beat JSON → Plot it. The curve draws itself and flags any leak (tension dropped but investment didn't rise to catch it). Load sample arc shows a finished example; + Add beat by hand if you'd rather rate it yourself.
  • 📋 Manuscript report — opens with the Clinic Verdict: graded dimensions tuned to your lane (horror and thrillers get a Dread grade; mysteries and thrillers get a Mystery Engine grade) and one readiness call, Revision Needed → Almost There → Polish-Ready. For the human-grade read, Copy "Overall Assessment" prompt and paste back the Editor's Letter your AI writes — it becomes the opening of your report. Every note you saved from a pass or audit is collected here too.
7

Export — and go back in time if you need to

⬇ Export in the top bar opens Take your work with you:

  • Word manuscript (.docx) — your accepted text in standard manuscript format, ready for Vellum, Atticus or your editor. Plain text manuscript (.txt) if you'd rather.
  • Manuscript report (.html) — verdict, scans, lab charts and every saved note as before/after cards. That file is your revision plan. (The same download sits at the bottom of the Manuscript report lab view, with a Copy master revision prompt beside it.)
  • Complete project backup (.json) — originals, decisions, notes and history. Reopen it on any computer with Open a backup (.json). Make one before you clear a browser or switch machines.
  • Editing history & audits (.txt) — the paper trail: which passes ran on which version, every revision, every audit report including your World Bible log and clue ledger.

🕘 Revision history (top right of the workspace) lists every accepted review and manual edit, newest first. ↩ Restore before puts a chapter back to how it was before that step — the current text is kept in history too, so nothing is ever lost. Imported originals are always one click away underneath.

💡 Need to remove a chapter or a whole book? Rename / delete chapter above the reader, or Rename / delete manuscript under the title — type DELETE in the box. Export a backup first if you might want it back.

The rhythm, once you know it

Import → settings once → pick a chapter → Start this pass → Copy prompt (or Run with AI) → paste the reply → Accept / Keep original → Finish review → next sweep. Repeat per chapter, run the whole-book audits as the book firms up (Clue & Fair-Play before you call a mystery done), glance at the Story lab as it grows, and export your Manuscript report when the verdict looks good. Your book gets the full checkup — you're still the doctor. 🩺

Quick answers

Is my book uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens in your browser and saves there automatically — there's no account and no server. Your words only travel when you paste a prompt into your own AI, or click Run with AI with a key you connected, in which case they go to that provider and nowhere else.

Do I have to paste results back into the tool?

For sweeps, yes — that's the whole point: the reply becomes decision cards, and Finish review is what updates your chapter so every later pass works on the newest version. Notes and audit reports are optional paste-backs; they only matter if you want them in your Manuscript report.

Suggestions, Revised chapter or Notes — which do I pick?

Suggestions nearly always. Replies are short, every edit arrives with its reason, and nothing can get cut off. Choose Revised chapter when you want the AI to do a heavy rewrite in one go, and Notes when you only want its opinion. Pick the format before you copy the prompt — the prompt changes with it.

What order should I run the passes?

Top to bottom works: the sweeps are numbered the way the class teaches them. Minimum viable checkup: Auto-Scan + Sweep 3 (Quality Check) + Sweep 9 (Line Edit) per chapter, the whole-book audits once the draft is complete, then the Overall Assessment at the end. Writing mystery or thriller? Run the Clue & Fair-Play Audit before you call the book done. Writing fantasy or sci-fi? World & Rules Consistency is your last gate. Writing horror? Keep an eye on the 🕯 Dread report as chapters stack up.

My AI said "response exceeded the output token maximum" (or just stopped mid-chapter)

That means one reply couldn't hold the whole revision — usually a very long chapter, or a book that loaded as one giant chapter because the splitter couldn't find your chapter headings (the clinic warns you on import when a chapter comes in over 12,000 words). Don't resend the same prompt — it fails the same way every time and eats your AI allowance. Instead: switch the format to Suggestions (short replies, no cut-offs); or make sure each chapter starts on its own line like "Chapter 1," delete the giant chapter, and import again. If you stay on Revised chapter, the prompt already tells your AI to deliver big revisions in parts — just say "NEXT" until you have them all, combine, and paste. The review panel also warns you when a pasted chapter is noticeably shorter than the loaded one.

I accepted something I shouldn't have

Right after Finish review, click Undo on the toast. Later, 🕘 Revision history → ↩ Restore before, or the Original tab → Restore this original to go all the way back to your import.
