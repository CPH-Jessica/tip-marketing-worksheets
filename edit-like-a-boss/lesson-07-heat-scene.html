Before & After Examples

Example 1 — Emotion Over Mechanics (Heat 4)

BEFORE:
He kissed her deeply. She moaned. He moved his hands over her body, feeling every curve. She arched into him, wanting more. The electricity between them was undeniable. Their bodies moved together in perfect rhythm.
AFTER:
He kissed the corner of her mouth first. Testing. She turned into it, caught his bottom lip between her teeth. He made a sound that was almost a laugh. Almost. His hands found her waist, then her ribs, then the place where her breath hitched and she forgot the clever thing she was going to say.

AI-isms removed. Personality added. Varied sentence structure. Push-pull pacing. Character voice intact.

Example 2 — Voice Consistency (Heat 3)

BEFORE (voice drops):
His touch ignited a fire within her soul. She surrendered to the overwhelming tide of passion that consumed her very being. In his arms, she found the home she had always been searching for.
AFTER (voice holds):
His thumb traced her collarbone and she thought, absurdly, about the grocery list on the counter. Then he kissed the spot his thumb had been and the grocery list evaporated. "You are thinking too hard," he said. "I am not thinking at all," she lied.

The character's personality stays present. She is still herself. The humor and the deflection are part of who she is.

Example 3 — Emotional Aftermath

BEFORE (ends flat):
They lay together, satisfied. He pulled her close and they both fell asleep, content and at peace.
AFTER (emotional landing):
He was tracing circles on her shoulder. She watched his hand and thought about how strange it was that this felt more intimate than anything that had come before it. She wanted to say something. She did not. He pressed his mouth to her hair and she felt him smile against it.

The aftermath reveals what changed. The quiet moment carries more weight than the scene itself.
