What You Are Building

You are building a custom AI reader persona — your Beta Bestie. By the end of this worksheet you will have a complete persona description you can save as a skill in Lesson 2 and use on every project from here forward.

Reminder: Your Beta Bestie is a READER, not an editor. She gives you reader reactions, not writing advice. She tells you what made her keep reading, what made her DNF, what made her text her group chat.

Beta Bestie vs. Slash Editor — Know the Difference

Slash Editor (Pass 7)Beta Bestie
Editor mindsetReader mindset
Asks: Does this scene EARN its place?Asks: Did this scene make me KEEP READING?
Structural feedbackEmotional feedback
Cuts what doesn't serve the storyFlags what doesn't satisfy the reader
Runs DURING the 8-pass systemRuns AFTER the 8-pass system
1

Identify Your Target Reader

Be specific. Generic reader profiles produce generic feedback.

2

Give Her a Name and a Vibe

The name and vibe make her feel like a person, not a prompt.

3

Define Her Opinions

These are the criteria she will use to evaluate your work.

4

Give Her Signature Phrases

Real phrases. The way she actually talks. This is what makes her feel like a person.

5

Define What She WON'T Do

Boundaries make her useful. Most AI personas are too nice. Yours shouldn't be.

Next Steps

  1. Copy the generated persona above
  2. Save it as a markdown file (e.g., your-beta-name.md)
  3. In Lesson 2, you will install this as a skill in your AI platform of choice (Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT, or Grok)
  4. Test her on a chapter you already have feedback on — compare her reactions to the real feedback
  5. Iterate. The first version is rarely sharp enough. Sharpen her over time.

Test Prompt — Once She's Installed

Use this prompt to put your Beta Bestie through her paces:

[BETA BESTIE NAME], I need you to read this chapter and give me your honest reader reaction. Pen name: [PEN NAME] Heat level: [1-5]/5 Where this chapter sits in the book: [Chapter X of Y / opening / mid / climax / etc.] [PASTE FULL CHAPTER HERE] Tell me: 1. Did you want to keep reading? Where exactly did you start to drift, if you did? 2. What made you react out loud (good or bad)? 3. What would you text your group chat about this chapter? 4. What would you say in a Goodreads review? 5. Would you pre-order the next book? Stay in character. Use your signature phrases. Be honest, not nice.
