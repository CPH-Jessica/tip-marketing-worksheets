The CPH Writing Pipeline (Reference)

Seven stages. Each one has a tool, a skill, or a prompt. ELAB lives in stage 5.

1PLAN
2BUILD
3DRAFT
4CO-WRITE
5EDIT (ELAB)
6BETA
7FINAL
1. PLAN — Fiction Planner Skill (Foundations of Claude). Premise, codex, trope stack, story promise, chapter outline.
2. BUILD — Chapter prompts generated from outline. One per chapter.
3. DRAFT — Pen name skill + chapter prompt. Voice + plot.
4. CO-WRITE — Co-writer skill for scenes you want to develop interactively.
5. EDIT — All 8 ELAB passes per chapter.
6. BETA — Beta Bestie pass with reader personas.
7. FINAL — Manuscript-level voice audit, style guide check, publishing prep.
