The Story Clinic's sister tool for translated books. Load your English original and its translation side by side, and get: instant offline parity checks (dropped text, untranslated English, name & number drift, typography), six translation passes for your AI (fidelity, naturalness, anglicisms, genre vocabulary, typography, glossary), and one exportable Translation Report with a ship-ready verdict. Editing the story itself? That's the Story Clinic — this tool assumes the book is done and checks the translation.

This is just a webpage — it builds prompts you copy. Paste them into a chat in the Claude Project (or custom GPT) that holds your book so your AI knows the story while it judges the translation. A plain chat works too.

Parity Scan runs offline · no AI

The mechanical half of translation QC, instant and free: dropped text, untranslated English, missing names and numbers, wrong quote marks. These are the failures machine translation commits most — and the easiest to prove.

Translation Passes — prompts for your AI

Six passes, each built from the Edit Like a Boss translator pipeline. Every prompt carries BOTH texts for the chapter pair you pick. ✏️ Edit asks your AI to fix the translation and return it with a change log; Review gives notes only. Paste the revised translation back under the pass and the next pass runs on the new version.

Target language, register, and style notes come from the Book Pair tab.

Names & Glossary offline consistency grid

Every recurring proper noun from the English source, counted in both texts. A name that appears 40 times in English and 0 times in the translation was renamed or lost — either is worth a look. Fill the "target term" column via the Glossary pass, paste the result below, and it rides along in your report.

Translation Verdict the clinic verdict

Every dimension the clinic measures, graded, with one call: ship it, revise it, or retranslate it. The offline verdict is a proxy — the passes give you the human-grade read.

Translation report

Paste back what your AI returned for each pass. Export report (top bar) saves the whole checkup — parity flags, ratio chart, glossary grid, verdict, and your pass notes — as one file on your computer.
