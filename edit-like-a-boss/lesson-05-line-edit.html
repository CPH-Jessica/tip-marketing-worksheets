The Line Edit Prompt

Copy this prompt. Replace the placeholders in gold with your project details. Paste your chapter where indicated.

You are line-editing a chapter for [PEN NAME], a [GENRE] author. Heat level: [1-5]/5. Here is the chapter to line edit: [PASTE FULL CHAPTER HERE] Run a LINE EDIT on this chapter. Check all 10 points: 1. ECHOES — Flag repeated words within 2-3 paragraphs (especially: eyes, heart, breath, gaze, chest, stomach). Replace with varied alternatives. 2. FILTER WORDS — Remove: felt, noticed, realized, seemed, watched, saw, heard, wondered, thought. Show the sensation directly instead. 3. WEAK VERBS — Replace was/were + -ing with active verbs. Kill "started to" and "began to." 4. ADVERB CRUTCHES — Cut -ly adverbs. Strengthen the verb instead. 5. DIALOGUE TAGS — Trim to "said/asked" or action beats. No "exclaimed, murmured, breathed" unless truly earned. 6. SENTENCE VARIETY — Flag strings of same-length sentences or repetitive structures. 7. OVERWRITING — Cut purple prose, redundant descriptions, metaphors that don't land. 8. FILLER PHRASES — Remove: "a little," "sort of," "kind of," "just," "really," "very," "that" (when unnecessary). 9. BODY LANGUAGE INVENTORY — Too many eyebrow raises, lip bites, jaw clenches? Vary the physical tells. 10. RHYTHM CHECK — Read dialogue aloud mentally. Does it flow or stumble? ALSO CHECK THE BANNED LIST: - Banned AI-isms: "electricity coursing through," "a smile tugging at," "couldn't help but," "found myself," "before I could stop myself," "let out a breath I didn't know I was holding," "something shifted," "I didn't trust my voice," "swallowed hard," "a beat of silence," "broke the silence," "search my face," "meet my gaze," "hold my gaze," "the air between us" - Banned words: suddenly, slowly, quietly, incredibly, entirely, finally, slightly, completely - Banned physical actions: arms crossed, eyes rolled, heart hammered, smirked - Banned dialogue tags: growled, whispered (overuse), softly, slightly - Never start with "[Name] + verb" repeatedly. Never start two consecutive paragraphs the same way. Return the revised chapter with all fixes applied, plus a change summary listing what was fixed and where.

Before & After Examples

Example 1 — Filter Words Removed

BEFORE:
She felt the anger rise in her chest. She noticed his jaw tighten. She realized he was not going to apologize. She watched him turn toward the door.
AFTER:
The anger rose hot in her chest. His jaw tightened. He was not going to apologize. He turned toward the door.

Four filter words removed (felt, noticed, realized, watched). Same beats, no distance between reader and experience.

Example 2 — Weak Verbs Strengthened

BEFORE:
She was walking toward the barn when she started to hear the sound of hooves. He was leaning against the fence, and he began to smile when he saw her.
AFTER:
She walked toward the barn. Hooves drummed somewhere beyond the tree line. He leaned against the fence and grinned when she rounded the corner.

Active verbs replace was/were + -ing. "Started to" and "began to" eliminated. Tighter, more immediate.

Example 3 — Echoes Fixed

BEFORE:
His eyes found hers across the room. She held his gaze for a beat, then looked away. When she looked back, his eyes had not moved. Something in his gaze made her breath catch.
AFTER:
He found her across the room. She held the look for a beat, then turned to the bar. When she glanced back, he had not moved. Something in the way he watched her made her breath catch.

"Eyes" x2 and "gaze" x2 in four sentences. Fixed with varied alternatives. Same tension, no echo.

Example 4 — AI-isms Replaced

BEFORE:
Electricity coursed through her when their hands touched. She couldn't help but lean closer. Something shifted in the air between them, and she let out a breath she didn't know she was holding.
AFTER:
His thumb grazed her wrist and her skin went warm. She leaned closer before she thought about it. The kitchen was too small for two people who were not going to kiss. She exhaled.

Four AI-isms replaced with specific, character-grounded details. "The kitchen was too small" does what "the air between them" could not.
