Who this is for

You finished a draft (that's the hard part!) and the word "editing" makes your stomach drop. The Coach walks you through the seven passes a real editor makes — one at a time, explained in plain English, never more than three fixes at once.

🌱 Open The Editing Coach
1

Read the welcome, click "I'm ready"

The first screen explains the two things that make the Coach different:

  • The promise: you'll never get more than three fixes at a time — no walls of red ink.
  • The rule: don't run your chapter through three different tools at once. You'll get three opinions and freeze. Finish this walk first.

Hit I'm ready — let's start →.

2

Fill in your book card

  • Book title — a working title is fine.
  • What do you write? — pick the closest genre; the Coach tailors its advice to it.
  • Your name (and pen name if you use one) — so the Coach can talk to you, and so your downloads come out labeled properly.
💡 Everything you type saves itself in your browser as you go. Close the tab, come back next week — your book is still there.
3

The voice card — optional, but lovely

Every prompt the Coach builds already tells the AI to protect your voice — your word choices, your rhythm, your quirks. Nothing to switch on. If you want the protection even sharper, describe your writing in the box. Two easy ways if you're stuck:

  • The thorough way: open ✍️ Your Style Sheet (linked right on the card), fill it in once, paste it in. You'll reuse it forever.
  • The two-minute way: after you've added a chapter, click 🎙 Discover my voice — the AI reads your writing and describes your voice to you. Paste its paragraph in the box.
4

Add chapter one

Scroll to Add a chapter, give it a name ("Chapter One" is perfect), paste the text, hit + Add this chapter. The Coach opens it and shows your seven steps.

💡 One chapter at a time is the whole philosophy. Finish this one, then come back for the next — you'll watch your book fill up with checkmarks.
5

Walk the seven steps, in order

The steps unlock one at a time, in the order a real editor works — shape first, sentences last:

  • 1. Does this chapter work? · 2. Do your characters feel real? · 3. Show me, don't tell me · 4. Does the dialogue sound like people? · 5. Does it flow? · 6. Polish the sentences · 7. Does it sound like AI?

Each step is the same little ritual:

  • Read What this step is and Why editors do it — that's the mini-lesson.
  • Hit 📋 Copy this step's prompt and paste it into your AI (Claude, ChatGPT — whichever you use). It comes back with what's working plus at most three things to try.
  • Stuck on a step that doesn't fit this chapter? skip this one is right there — no guilt, come back anytime.
6

Bring the answer back — two doors

At the end of every prompt, the AI asks whether you'd like it to rewrite the chapter or leave the changes to you. Whichever you choose, bring the answer home:

  • 📝 Keep the notes — paste the three fixes and hit Save these notes. They go into your running editing notes so you can work at your own pace.
  • 📄 Paste the updated chapter — whether the AI rewrote it or you did, paste it and hit Show me what changed →. You'll see every edit — what goes, what comes in — and accept or ignore each change. Nothing is saved until you say so, and there's an ↩ Undo if you change your mind.
✍️ Before you move on, the Coach asks for one line: what did you change, and why? That's not busywork — writing it down is how the lesson sticks, and it all lands in your editing notes. Then Mark this step done →.
7

The celebration — and your downloads

Finish step seven and you get the 🎉 card — because seven passes is genuinely what a developmental editor walks through, and you just did it yourself.

  • ⬇ Download my edited chapter — your newest version as a clean text file.
  • 📄 Download my editing notes — everything you learned this chapter: each step, the notes you kept, what you changed and why. That document is your own personal editing course, written by you.
  • Next chapter → — back to your book. Paste the next one and go again.
🎓 After you've finished two chapters, a "Ready for more?" card appears — that's your invitation to the full Story Clinic, the advanced version of what you've been doing here: every pass, whole-book graphs, and a readiness verdict.

The rhythm, once you know it

Open a step → read the little lesson → copy the prompt into your AI → bring back three fixes → make the changes → write one line about why → next step. Seven steps per chapter, one chapter at a time. Slow is smooth, and smooth is how books get finished. 🌱

Quick answers

Is my book uploaded anywhere?

No. The Coach is just a webpage — your chapters live in your browser on your computer. The only time your words travel is when you paste a prompt into your own AI.

Which AI do I need?

Any of them. Claude and ChatGPT both work beautifully — the prompts are written so any assistant knows exactly what to do (and what not to do to your voice).

Why are the later steps locked?

Because order is the secret. There's no point polishing a sentence in a scene you might cut. The Coach holds the order so you don't have to think about it — and if a step truly doesn't apply, "skip this one" unlocks the next.

The AI gave me a huge wall of notes anyway?

Make sure you used the copied prompt rather than asking freehand — the prompt caps feedback at three fixes and orders the AI to lead with what's working. If it still rambles, tell it: "just the three, please."

When am I ready for the Story Clinic?

When the seven steps start feeling familiar — usually two or three chapters in. The Clinic is the same philosophy with the training wheels off: 12+ passes, whole-book lab reports, and a readiness verdict for your finished manuscript.
