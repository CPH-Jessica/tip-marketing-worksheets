Quality Checkpoints

Mark these as you hit each milestone. Do not proceed past a checkpoint until it passes.

After Pass 3: Logic Test

Can you read the entire book without any moment where you think "that does not make sense"?

After Pass 5: Dialogue Test

Cover the dialogue tags. Can you tell who is speaking just by how they talk?

After Pass 7: Pacing Test

Is there any scene you would skip as a reader? Be honest.

After Pass 8: Voice Test

Does the last chapter sound like the first chapter? Is the voice consistent throughout?

Manuscript-Level Audit

After all 8 passes on every chapter, zoom out and check the book as a whole.

Overall Pacing

Does the pacing feel right across the full arc? No sagging middle?

Character Arc Resolution

Do the arcs resolve satisfyingly, both logically AND emotionally?

Heat/Tension Progression

Is the escalation pattern right? Does each scene build on the last?

Unresolved Threads

Any subplots, promises, or setups that never pay off?

Series Setup (if applicable)

Are series hooks planted naturally? Do they create anticipation without cliffhangers?
