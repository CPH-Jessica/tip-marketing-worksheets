What Pass 9 Is (And Isn't)

Pass 9 is YOUR final pass on every book. Always. Every project. It is non-negotiable — there is no version of this system where the AI does all the work and you just hit publish.

Within YOUR Pass 9, you may also decide to bring in outside human help (a paid copy editor or proofreader). That's a business decision. We cover it in the second half of this worksheet. But YOU are always the primary 9th pass. The paid editor is OPTIONAL extra muscle.

Part 1 — YOUR Pass 9

Project Setup

1The Read-Aloud Pass

Read the whole manuscript out loud (or via text-to-speech). On a different device than you edited on. Print it if you can.

2Emotional Gut Check

Did you FEEL anything reading this? Honest answer.

Where did I disengage?
What didn't I FEEL that I was supposed to feel?
What would my #1 fan flag?

3Brand Check

Does this book deliver what readers of THIS pen name expect? You are the brand authority — the AI follows your skill, but YOU verify the skill is doing its job.

4Reader Handoff Test

If you handed this to your #1 fan right now, what would she text back?

5Final Decision Call

Only YOU can answer this. Not your editor. Not your beta. Not your AI.

Part 2 — Do You Also Need a Paid Editor?

Within YOUR Pass 9 — The Paid Editor Decision

This section is OPTIONAL. Pass 9 is always YOU. Sometimes — when the project justifies it — you also bring in outside human help. Use the questions below to decide for THIS book.

The 5-Question Decision Framework

Two or more YES answers = hire a paid editor. Mostly NO = YOUR Pass 9 is enough.

Question 1: Publishing Path

Is this going trad, or to an imprint that requires "professionally edited"?

Question 2: Launch Profile

Tier-one launch — paid ads, BookTok push, six-figure expectation?

Question 3: New Pen Name or Genre

First book in a new pen name, or first time in this genre?

Question 4: Style Guide Maturity

Is your pen name style guide STILL EVOLVING (not yet confident the AI consistently lands where you want it)?

Question 5: ROI Math

Will this book earn enough lifetime revenue to justify a $1,500-$5,000 editorial investment?

Vetting Checklist (Before You Hire)

Integrating Edits Back

The integration rule:
  • Accept without overthinking: Grammar, punctuation, spelling
  • Question every style change: "Does this sound MORE or LESS like my pen name?"
  • Reject anything off-brand even if "technically better"
  • If rejection rate >40%: Wrong editor OR rigid style guide — diagnose before next project

When YOUR Pass 9 Is Enough (Skip the Paid Editor)

The paid editor is a BUSINESS decision, not a moral imperative.

Skip the paid editor for:

  • Rapid-release books in an established series with loyal readers
  • Box sets and reissues of previously-edited material
  • Free reader magnets / novellas (list growth, not revenue)
  • Backlist AI-refresh relaunches
  • Test books in a new pen name (ship first, invest if data justifies it)

For these, YOUR Pass 9 plus the 8 AI passes is the complete editing system. Ship and move on.
