The Quality Check Prompt

Copy this prompt. Replace the placeholders in gold with your project details. Paste your chapter where indicated.

You are editing a chapter for [PEN NAME], a [GENRE] author. Heat level: [1-5]/5. Here is the chapter outline / chapter prompt for this chapter: [PASTE YOUR CHAPTER OUTLINE HERE] Here is the chapter to edit: [PASTE YOUR FULL CHAPTER HERE] Run a QUALITY CHECK on this chapter. Check all 11 points: 1. NATURAL PROSE — Does it read like a human wrote it? Flag AI-isms, overused adverbs, repeated words/phrases. Check against the banned list. 2. TONE MATCH — Is the humor/intensity genre-appropriate for this pen name? 3. OUTLINE ADHERENCE — Did this chapter stick to the chapter prompt ONLY? No veering into later plot points, no premature resolution, no speeding the book. 4. ARC CONSISTENCY — Does it follow the character/story arc from the outline? 5. ROMANTIC/EMOTIONAL PROGRESSION — Is the relationship or emotional tension advancing appropriately for this chapter's position? 6. PACING & TRANSITIONS — Are scene shifts smooth and earned, not rushed or jarring? 7. SHOW VS. TELL — Flag any moments that tell emotion instead of showing it through action, dialogue, or sensation. 8. CHAPTER ENDING — Does it end on a hook with forward momentum? No foreshadowing. Ends ON the storybeat, not after it. 9. POV CONFIRMATION — Single POV throughout? No head-hopping? 10. HEAT LEVEL CHECK — Does the heat match the pen name's calibration? 11. BANNED LIST CHECK — No banned names (Chen, Lucian, Marcus, Emma), no banned AI-isms ("electricity coursing," "a smile tugging," "couldn't help but," "found myself," "let out a breath I didn't know I was holding," "something shifted," etc.), no banned physical actions (arms crossed, eyes rolled, heart hammered, smirked). IMPORTANT: If ANY check fails, REVISE the chapter to fix the issue. Do not just flag problems — fix them. Then return: 1. The revised chapter 2. A short summary of what was changed and why

Before & After Examples

Example 1 — AI-ism Sweep

BEFORE:
She suddenly felt a wave of emotion crash over her. Something shifted in the air between them. She couldn't help but notice the way his eyes searched her face, and she let out a breath she didn't know she was holding.
AFTER:
Her throat tightened. He was watching her — not her eyes, the hollow at the base of her throat where her pulse gave her away. She swallowed and looked at the menu.

5 AI-isms eliminated. Same emotional beat preserved with specific, physical detail.

Example 2 — Show vs. Tell

BEFORE (TELL):
Jake felt angry about what she'd said. He realized he needed to leave before he said something he'd regret.
AFTER (SHOW):
Jake set his fork down. Carefully. Lined it up parallel to the knife. "I'm going to go check on the truck." He didn't look at her on the way out.

No filter words (felt, realized). Action and restraint show the emotion.

Example 3 — Chapter Ending

BEFORE (resolves):
She closed her eyes and smiled. Everything was going to be okay. She could feel it. For the first time in weeks, she felt at peace.
AFTER (momentum):
She pulled into the driveway and killed the engine. His truck was there. The porch light was on. And the front door was open.

No resolution. No foreshadowing. Ends ON the beat with forward momentum.
