1

Set up your patient file

  • Type your book title (it names your report).
  • Pick the voice preset closest to your book — rom-com, small-town, romantasy, dark, Regency, paranormal. Every prompt the tool builds is calibrated to this voice.
  • Have your own voice skill from class? Pick Custom, click the little button in that box, paste that prompt into your Claude — it distills your voice skill into perfect voice notes. Paste the result back.
  • Set your heat level honestly (1 closed door → 5 fully explicit). The passes hold your book to it.
💡 Where do the prompts go? The clinic is just a webpage — it builds prompts you copy. Paste them into a chat inside the Claude Project that holds your book files for the sharpest results. A plain chat works too.
2

Load your manuscript — one button

  • Paste your whole book or just chapter one into the big box and hit ▶ Go. Whole books split into chapters automatically.
  • Working chapter by chapter? After the first Go, the button becomes ▶ Go — add next chapter. Paste, press, repeat. (+ Add next chapter sits right beside it if you prefer clicking exactly what you mean.)
  • Need a clean slate? ↺ Start over lives in the top bar — it wipes everything, so hit Save file first if you want a backup.
💡 Try Load demo chapter first — it's deliberately riddled with problems so you can watch every feature fire before trusting it with your baby.
3

Read your Auto-Scan

The instant you load a chapter, the scan flags banned words, AI-isms, dramatic triplets, flash-forwards ("she would later realize…"), filter words, and weak verbs — offline and free. Nothing uploads, ever.

  • Red (critical) first, always — those are the "AI wrote this" tells.
  • Click Show highlighted text to see every hit lit up inside your actual prose.
4

Run a pass — or just say "fix it"

  • On the Editing Passes tab, pick your chapter under Apply to.
  • Open any pass. The gold ✏️ Copy EDIT prompt — fix it for me makes your AI actually edit the chapter and hand back: the revised text, a change log, and a short "needs author" list for decisions only you can make.
  • Prefer to make every call yourself? Use Copy review prompt — notes only.
  • The audit passes (Plot Holes, Timeline, Arc Pacing) are review-only on purpose — they flag; the story decisions are yours.
5

Paste the revision back — the victory lap

Right under the same pass is a 📥 paste-back box. Drop your AI's revised chapter in and hit Update chapter & re-scan:

  • The chapter updates in place — watch your flag count drop (that's your proof the fix landed).
  • Then hit 👀 View tracked changes — every edit Word-style: removals struck through in red, additions in green, with a running "+adds / −cuts" count. Flip through at speed instead of reading a change log; it's computed on your computer, no AI involved.
  • The next pass you copy is automatically built from the new version. Dev edit → update → dialogue pass → update. Your changes carry forward the whole way, no tab-hopping.
💡 There's also a 🔁 Paste revised text button inside each chapter card on the first tab, for when you're over there.
6

Check your lab results

The Story Reports tab computes five whole-book reports right in your browser:

  • 📈 Plot — your emotional arc. ~90% of bestsellers reach both tinted bands: one real high, one real low.
  • 🎭 Character — your cast, auto-detected: presence bars per chapter, confusable names, characters who vanish, and your dialogue share vs. the 25–35% bestseller range.
  • ⏱ Pacing — where readers speed up and where they put the book down. Watch the middle third.
  • ⚔️ Conflict — chapters that have gone quiet (tension on every page is the rule).
  • 🕰 Timeline — your story's clock, read from phrases like "the next morning": floating chapters, time skips, whirlwind-romance math.

Each report also has a deep-dive prompt for your AI and a paste-back box that feeds your final report.

7

Map your satisfaction curve

The Satisfaction tab is the Charge Model from class: tension released should convert into bond earned. Copy the "rate my beats" prompt, let your AI score the whole book, paste the JSON back — the curve draws itself and flags any leak (a payoff where tension dropped but bond didn't rise to catch it).

8

Get your verdict & download the report

  • The Manuscript Report tab opens with the Clinic Verdict — seven graded dimensions, one readiness call: Revision Needed → Almost There → Polish-Ready.
  • Want the human-grade read? Copy the Overall Assessment prompt — your AI writes a full editor's letter: strengths, ranked concerns, and the one thing to fix first. Paste it back.
  • Hit ⬇ Download Manuscript report — the whole checkup saves to your computer: verdict, priorities, every fix as a before/after card, all your graphs. That file is your revision plan.

The rhythm, once you know it

Paste chapter → Go → read the scan → EDIT prompt into Claude → paste revision back → next pass. Repeat per chapter, glance at the lab reports as the book grows, download your Manuscript Report when the verdict looks good. Your book gets the full checkup — you're still the doctor. 🩺

Quick answers

Is my book uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens in your browser and saves there automatically. The only time your words travel is when you paste a prompt into your own AI.

Do I have to paste results back into the tool?

Only if you want them in your downloadable report. The paste-back that matters is the revised chapter (step 5) — that keeps every later pass working on your newest version.

What order should I run the passes?

Top to bottom works: they're numbered the way the class teaches them. Minimum viable checkup: Auto-Scan + Pass 1 (Quality Check) + Pass 4 (Line Edit) per chapter, then the Overall Assessment at the end.

My AI said "response exceeded the output token maximum" (or just stopped mid-chapter)

That means one reply couldn't hold the whole revision — usually a very long chapter, or a book that loaded as one giant chapter because the splitter couldn't find your chapter headings. Don't resend the same prompt — it fails the same way every time and eats your AI allowance. Instead: check the chapter list (a ⚠ long tag marks the oversized ones), make sure each chapter starts on its own line like "Chapter 1," and re-copy the EDIT prompt — it now tells your AI to deliver big revisions in parts (just say "next" until you have them all, combine, and paste back).

Something's confusing or broken?

That's what the 💬 Feedback button in the tool's top bar is for — your notes go straight to Jessica AND the Claude that builds this tool. Beta feedback built half the features on this page.
