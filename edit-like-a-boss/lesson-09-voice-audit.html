Pass 8: Voice Audit

The final polish. Does chapter 20 sound like chapter 1?
Edit Like a Boss | The Invisible Pen

What this pass catches: Voice drift across the manuscript, AI-generic passages, inconsistency with your pen name brand, tone/heat/vocabulary mismatches, and the gradual smoothing that happens when AI overwrites your voice with its own default register.

Why it is last: Everything else is fixed. Content is final. Now we calibrate the voice.

Step 3: Learn to Spot AI Drift

Study these before/after examples. Train your eye to catch AI-generic prose.

Example 1: Emotional Beat

AI-Drifted

"She felt a complex mix of emotions as she considered the implications of his words. Part of her wanted to trust him, while another part remained cautious. The tension between them was palpable, yet she found herself drawn to his quiet strength."

Moxie Savage Voice

"His words hit like a backhand. Trust him? She wanted to laugh. She wanted to bolt. Instead she held his stare and let the silence do the talking. He did not flinch. Neither did she."

Example 2: Thoughtful Gesture

AI-Drifted

"She appreciated his thoughtful gesture, feeling warmth spread through her at his consideration. Their connection deepened with each interaction, and she realized she was beginning to trust him."

Sage Kennedy Voice

"'You got me a hot dog from the rink stand.' She stared at it. 'You remembered I hate the fancy ones.' 'Ketchup and mustard. No relish. I pay attention.' She bit into it before he could see her face do something embarrassing."

Example 3: Setting Description

AI-Drifted

"The forest was dark and mysterious, creating an atmosphere of ancient power. She could feel the magic in the air around her, and it made her both afraid and fascinated."

Ruthie Clark Voice

"The canopy swallowed the last of the light three turns back. Now the dark had teeth. It pressed against her skin like something alive, tasting her pulse, deciding whether she belonged here. The magic was not in the air. It was the air. Every breath she drew carried it deeper into her lungs, into her blood, into the marrow of her."

The Formula: AI drift = abstract + balanced + hedged. Your voice = specific + committed + distinctive. When you see "complex mix of emotions" or "their connection deepened," that is the AI's default register overwriting your pen name.

Step 4: The Voice Audit Prompt

Copy this prompt. Fill in the bracketed fields. Paste your voice profile and chapters. Run it on every chapter, starting with the ones from the middle of your manuscript (where drift is worst).

You are running a VOICE AUDIT on a chapter for [PEN NAME], a [GENRE] author. Heat level: [1-5]/5. Here is the pen name voice profile / writing style guide: [PASTE VOICE PROFILE OR DESCRIBE: vocabulary, sentence rhythms, heat calibration, humor level, tone markers, what this pen name MUST have and MUST NOT have] Here is Chapter 1 or an early chapter for voice baseline (optional but recommended): [PASTE EARLY CHAPTER FOR COMPARISON, OR SKIP] Here is the chapter to audit: [PASTE CHAPTER HERE] Run a VOICE AUDIT. Check these areas: 1. VOICE CONSISTENCY — Does this chapter sound like the same author as Chapter 1? Flag any drift toward generic, literary, or AI-smoothed prose. 2. TONE CALIBRATION — Is the humor/intensity/darkness ratio correct for this pen name? Not too light, not too dark, not too neutral. 3. HEAT LEVEL MATCH — Does the heat level (language, explicitness, emotional intensity) match the pen name's calibration throughout? 4. VOCABULARY CHECK — Is the word choice consistent with the pen name's register? No modern slang in Regency. No literary prose in rom-com. No sweetness in dark romance. 5. SENTENCE RHYTHM — Does the prose rhythm match the pen name? Short brutal sentences for thriller. Lush flowing sentences for romantasy. Snappy for rom-com. 6. INTERNAL MONOLOGUE FLAVOR — Does the POV character's inner voice match the pen name brand? Pop culture refs for Sage Kennedy. Raw intensity for Moxie Savage. Deadpan wit for Kelsey Croft. 7. AI DRIFT DETECTION — Flag any passages that sound "AI-smooth" — too polished, too balanced, too generic. These are the spots where the AI overwrote the author's voice. 8. GENRE PROMISE — Would a reader of this pen name feel satisfied? Would they buy the next book? PEN-NAME-SPECIFIC CHECKS (run the applicable set): [Include whichever pen name applies — Sage Kennedy humor/banter/heat checks, Constance warmth/community checks, Ruthie lush/atmosphere checks, Marjorie period/wit checks, Kelsey snark/comedy checks, Moxie intensity/possessive checks, etc.] Return: - Voice Audit Report with Target Voice, Current Voice assessment, Drift Detected (yes/no with specifics) - Specific passages flagged with explanations of what drifted and why - The revised chapter with voice corrections applied
