Three Names, One Program

KDP Select

What you, the author, enroll your ebook in. 90 days at a time. It renews automatically unless you turn that off.

Kindle+

What readers subscribe to. It was called Kindle Unlimited until October 22, 2026.

KDP Select Global Fund

The monthly pot of money that pays enrolled authors for pages read, and now pages listened to.

PAUSE AND DO: after the "Ask me how I know" slide

Exclusivity Sweep

Before you enroll, find every place this ebook has ever been. Amazon requires the ebook to be exclusive, and an honest mistake still counts.

Tip: Exclusivity covers the ebook only. Your paperback and hardcover can be sold anywhere.

Four Honest Questions

Answer all four to see which way you're leaning.

What Changed With Audiobooks

Virtual voice audiobooks

If your ebook is in KDP Select, its virtual voice audiobook is in Kindle+ automatically. You can't have one in without the other.

ACX audiobooks

Can be enrolled if they meet Amazon's requirements. Lesson 7 covers them.

Where

Audiobooks in Kindle+ are in the US store only for now.

My Decision

Amazon's rules as of November 2026. Amazon changes these, so confirm on KDP Help before you act.
