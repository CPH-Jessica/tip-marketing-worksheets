Tip: A simple series pattern: free days on book one this period, a Countdown Deal on book two next period.

The Two KDP Select Tools

Free Book Promotion

Up to 5 days per 90-day period, all at once or spread out. Schedule at least one day ahead. Unused days don't roll over. You earn no royalty on free downloads.

Kindle Countdown Deal

A discount with a timer, from one hour up to 7 days. US and UK stores only. Enrolled at least 30 days first. It has to end at least 14 days before your period ends. You keep your royalty rate at the lower price.
