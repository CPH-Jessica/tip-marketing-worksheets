The Two Doors, Side by Side

Virtual Voice (inside KDP)

Computer narration. Free to create during the beta. By invitation, US store only. You set a price from $3.99 to $14.99 and earn a 40 percent royalty.

ACX (human narration)

Your own recording or a narrator you hire. Under the new model: 50 percent if exclusive to Audible, 30 percent if not.

Audiobooks in Kindle+

Virtual voice: in automatically when the ebook is in KDP Select. ACX: can be enrolled if it's exclusive to Audible, made with Pay for Production, has Read & Listen on, and the ebook is in KDP Select.
