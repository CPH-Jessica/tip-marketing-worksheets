Pre-Order Setup Checklist

The Rules, in One Place

How far ahead

Up to 18 months, for pre-orders created on or after September 2, 2026.

Final file deadline

At least 72 hours before release. For your first-ever KDP book, 5 days, and you upload a manuscript when you create the pre-order.

If you miss it or cancel

First time: a one-time courtesy for your account. Second time: no new pre-orders for 6 months, across your whole catalog.

Print books

KDP pre-orders are for ebooks. Paperbacks and hardcovers can have a scheduled release date instead.

Amazon's rules as of November 2026. Amazon changes these, so confirm on KDP Help before you act.
