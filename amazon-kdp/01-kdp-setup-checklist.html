Publishing More Than One Book?

The weekly limit

Since September 2026, KDP allows two new titles per format each week: two ebooks, two paperbacks, two hardcovers. It resets on Sunday. If you're launching a series all at once, spread it across weeks.

The AI question

On the Content tab, Amazon asks whether your book includes AI-generated content. Read Amazon's definitions on that page and answer honestly.

Amazon's rules as of November 2026. Amazon changes these, so confirm on KDP Help before you act.
