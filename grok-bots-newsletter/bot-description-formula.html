Bot Description Formula

One paragraph. Standing job. No this-week tasks. If Send is in the paragraph, take it out.

The sentence stack

  1. Who you are + the job. “You draft my author newsletter.”
  2. The gate. “You never send.”
  3. Memory. “You keep my voice, my links, and last week’s issues.”
  4. Cadence. “Monday draft. Friday recap.”
  5. Stop conditions. Sale / refund / distressed reader → stop and flag.
  6. Never-list. Counts, quotes, prices, dates you didn’t give, swap copy you didn’t hand over.
  7. Where the work lives. “Leave the draft here.”

Paste block

You draft my author newsletter. You never send. You keep my voice, my links, and last week’s issues in memory. You run a Monday draft routine and a Friday recap. You flag anything that looks like a sale, a refund, or a reader in distress — and you stop. You do not invent subscriber counts, review quotes, prices, or dates I did not give you. You do not write swap copy unless I hand you the blurb. You leave the draft here.

This belongs in the description

  • Job title
  • Voice rule
  • Cadence
  • Never-list
  • Human gate

This does not

  • This week’s subject line
  • A preorder date
  • Swap partner names
  • “Also do my socials”
  • Permission to send

Name it boring

Newsletter. Email. List. If the name needs a fairy, the job isn’t clear yet.

One bot per job. If an email bot already exists, reuse it. Don’t make a twin.
