Q4 Holiday Release Workshop

Map your Halloween → Autumn → Christmas releases and end the year strong
Ran Live July 29, 2026 · Full Replay Coming

Holiday readers are the most predictable buyers of the year — they show up on schedule, they search by season, and they binge everything on the shelf. The authors who win Q4 aren't the ones who write faster. They're the ones who planned in July.

In this live, hands-on workshop we'll map your entire fourth quarter together: which holiday waves fit your books, which tropes readers are hunting for in each one, and a week-by-week marketing plan working backward from your release dates.

The Three Waves of Q4

You don't need all three. You need the right one (or two) for your catalog — that's what we'll figure out together.

🎃

Spooky Season

Readers buy Sept 1 – Oct 31
Release by Oct 1
🍂

Cozy Autumn

Readers buy Sept 15 – Nov 25
Release by Oct 20
🎄

Christmas & Holiday

Readers buy Oct 1 – Dec 20
Release by Nov 15

What You'll Leave With

  • Your Q4 release map — one, two, or three themed releases matched to the buy windows above (novellas and quick reads absolutely count).
  • A holiday trope stack for each book — the seasonal hook + core tropes readers actually type into the search bar.
  • A backward-planned marketing timeline — drafting, cover, ARCs, swaps, newsletter, and launch-week beats with real dates on the calendar.
  • A commitment you can actually keep — we'll size your plan to your real writing pace, not a fantasy one.
