Five Classes: We Write It Together

October 8 is class one of the five-week write-along. In each class, we work on our Christmas novellas together, with 45 minutes of protected writing or revision and optional screen-sharing coaching with Jessica.

  • Week 1: choose your novella and start writing — Thursday, October 8, 1:00–2:30pm ET.
  • Weeks 2–5: write, get help, and keep going — Wednesdays, October 14, 21, 28 and November 4, all 1:00–2:30pm ET. Each class is 90 minutes.
  • Optional coaching and progress sharing — share a relevant screen window, read a short passage aloud, describe a sticking point, or keep writing privately. Our daily progress thread welcomes words, revised scenes, or minutes worked.
  • Our draft target — a Christmas novella draft by November 4, including the final class's writing time.
  • Your release date is your choice — allow time after drafting for revision, editing, proofreading, formatting, cover work, and platform review. There is no shared upload or release deadline.
