Q4 Holiday Release Workshop

Holiday readers search in season-speak. They don't look for "a romance in October" — they type snowed in grumpy sunshine Christmas romance. A holiday book is a normal trope stack wearing a seasonal costume: one seasonal wrapper (the holiday hook) plus two or three core tropes (the relationship engine).

Work one wave at a time below. Your picks save automatically in this browser, and every completed wave lands in Your Q4 Lineup at the bottom — print it, or send it straight into the Q4 Marketing Planner when you're done.

Step 5

The Costume Test

The single biggest reason holiday books flop: the season is decoration, not plot. Readers can smell a wreath glued onto a regular book. Answer honestly — this is the part that makes the book actually seasonal.
