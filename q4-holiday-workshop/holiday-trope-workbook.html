Q4 Holiday Release Workshop

Holiday readers search in season-speak. They don't look for "a romance in October" — they type snowed in grumpy sunshine Christmas romance. A holiday book is a normal trope stack wearing a seasonal costume: one seasonal wrapper (the holiday hook) plus two or three core tropes (the relationship engine).

Work one wave at a time below. Your picks save automatically in this browser, and every completed wave lands in Your Q4 Lineup at the bottom — print it when you're done.
