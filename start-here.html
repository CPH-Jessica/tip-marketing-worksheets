L1 · Beginner Writer

Let's build your foundation

You haven't published yet, the words trope and blurb are fuzzy, and you've dabbled with AI but nothing serious. Perfect. You're exactly who this was built for.
Your first three steps
  1. Watch the Live Call Replays.Get the lay of the land — my background, the “AI as partner” philosophy, and the 5-Phase Author Roadmap so you can see the whole journey before you start walking it.
  2. Take “L1 Class: Understanding Tropes.”Before you write a single word, learn to speak reader language. This is the difference between a book that sells and one that sits.
  3. Start “Pen Name Launcher — The Complete System.”Six lessons that walk you from naming your pen name all the way to launch week — newsletter, reader magnet, cover, KDP, the works.
You're ready for L2 when… you've drafted (or nearly) your first book and the basic pieces — pen name, newsletter, cover, blurb — aren't scary anymore, even if they're rough.
L2 · Newly Published

Make AI yours — and learn to sell the book

You've got a book out, you're new to actually drafting with AI, you have a few tools but aren't sure how to use them, and marketing feels like a foreign language. Let's fix all three.
Your first three steps
  1. Set up your Claude: “Foundations of Claude — Before You Prompt,” then Part 1.The fastest way to stop wondering if AI is “cheating” is to make it sound like YOU. That starts with teaching it who you are.
  2. Grab the Free Resources + “How to Build Custom Gems for Blurbs.”The Banned List, the blurb checkers, and the Gems guide give you a real handle on keywords, blurbs, and the marketing that actually moves books.
  3. Watch “How I Use AI Tools to Edit” and try the Beta Reader Bestie skill.See the whole process — brainstorm to edit — and put a brutally honest beta reader to work on your pages.
You're ready for L3 when… you've made peace that AI is a real partner, you can use your core tools, and you've got a basic marketing handle on your published book.
L3 · Established & AI-Curious

Get it to sound like YOU

You've got a backlist, you're folding AI into a process that already works, and you may not want readers or peers knowing yet. The headline goal here is simple: make the words sound unmistakably like you.
Your first three steps
  1. Take “How to Give Claude a Brain.”Claude Projects — Instructions, Knowledge, Conversations. This is the efficiency unlock most established authors have backwards, and it changes everything.
  2. Build your voice: “Foundations Part 2 — Building Skills” + the Fiction Ghostwriter Skill Builder.This is the “sound like me” work. You build it once, and Claude carries your voice into every session after.
  3. Run “Edit Like a Boss” (the 9-pass system).Edits that protect your voice instead of flattening it. Add the Slasher Editor skill when you're ready to cut hard.
You're ready for L4 when… AI is woven into your workflow (projects, instructions, a dialed-in voice skill) and you trust the output is genuinely your voice.
L4 · Competent & Optimizing

Sharpen, scale, stay ahead

You can run the whole pipeline. Now it's about refining your systems, scaling across pen names, and keeping pace as the tools change. (This is where I live, too.)
Where to point your energy
  1. “Writing Spicy with AI” + the heat workflow.The thing most tools won't help you with — and one of our real differentiators. Lean in.
  2. Scale with the Premium Skill Packs + Story Studio.Series Architect, Market Compass, Plot Engine, and a dashboard to keep every pen name straight without losing your mind.
  3. Go advanced: multi-LLM, the Translator, and the live cohort calls.Route work across tools, open new markets, and bring your real problems to the calls — that's where the best stuff happens.
This is the mastery tier. The goal isn't to “finish” — it's to keep getting sharper. If you want direct time with me, the Penthouse cohort is where that lives.
