4 · Shorts Strategy

Two per week, cut from the week's long video — zero extra filming. Under 60 seconds, hook in the first line, one idea each.

The Repeatable Formats
1. "Banned word of the week" — read an AI cliché dramatically, then the fix. (Endless series, feeds the Banned Words tool.) 2. "One-pass tip" — a single editing pass explained in 45 seconds, points to the 9-pass video. 3. "Trope stack or flop?" — react to a trope stack (from comments!), pass/fail with one fix. 4. "Before/after" — a robotic AI paragraph vs. the same paragraph after your style sheet. Screen-record, voiceover. 5. "Ads furnace check" — one readiness check per Short.
Shorts Description Template
[One-line hook restating the tip] Full video: [link to the week's long video] Free tools & classes: https://payhip.com/theinvisiblepen #AIforAuthors #WritingCommunity #SelfPublishing #BookTok
Comment-mining loop: the pinned comments on the launch videos are designed to generate material — banned words, trope stacks, failed checks. Every good reply becomes a Short. The audience writes your content calendar for you.
