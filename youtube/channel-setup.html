Avatar / Profile Photo — 800 × 800 px
Option A (recommended): your headshot on a cream background with a thin coral ring — faces outperform logos for small channels. Option B: gold pen-nib mark on navy, "TIP" in cream serif beneath it.

Gold pen nib on transparent background, or a coral "SUBSCRIBE" pill in cream text. Display time: entire video.
Thumbnail system (so the channel page looks cohesive from day one): navy background · 3–5 word hook in big cream serif · one word highlighted coral · your face right third, cropped from the chest up · gold nib in a corner as the brand mark.
