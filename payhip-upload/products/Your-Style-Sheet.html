7Anchor paragraphs

Two paragraphs of your own writing that sound the MOST like you — not your best plot, your truest voice. The bit where a reader would say "oh, that's her." No published prose yet? Write four honest sentences about your morning. Your voice shows up anywhere.

6My banned list

The clichés and tics that are NOT you. Forbid them by name — the machine can obey a named ban, it can't obey "don't be cheesy." A real working author's sheet bans "no tangled sheets or slick folds." Specific. Named.
