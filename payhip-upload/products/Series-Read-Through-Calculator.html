Series Read-Through Calculator

Know what a Book 1 reader is actually worth

Series read-through is the single most important metric for series authors. It tells you what percentage of readers who finish Book 1 go on to buy Book 2, Book 3, and beyond.

Why this matters: If you only look at Book 1 sales, you'll think your ads are losing money. Factor in read-through, and a "losing" campaign on Book 1 might actually be your biggest profit center. This calculator does the math for you.

Enter sales and KENP reads for each book over the same time period (last 30 days, last 90 days, or lifetime — your call, just be consistent).

Where do I find these numbers?

Sales: KDP Reports → Royalties Report → filter by book and date range. The "Net Units Sold" column.

KENP Reads: KDP Reports → KENP Read Report. Pages read for the same period.

Tip: Use the same time window for every book in the series. If you can, pick a 90-day window where all books were already released and selling — that gives the cleanest read-through math.

What This Means For Your Ads

Romance Industry Benchmarks

Rough benchmarks for romance series. Other genres vary — thriller and fantasy series often see lower B1 to B2 conversion but stronger retention once readers commit.

Read-Through (B1 → B2)What It Means
70%+Excellent. You can run aggressive Book 1 ads and stay profitable.
50–69%Solid. Most successful romance series live here. Ads work with good targeting.
30–49%Concerning. Either Book 1 isn't hooking readers or the series isn't being marketed as a series.
Under 30%Problem. Book 1 ending or Book 2 cover/blurb may be losing readers. Investigate before scaling ads.
