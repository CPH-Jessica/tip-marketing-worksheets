Meet Ember

Your heat-scene writer. She writes the scene. All of it. On the page. At YOUR heat level.

What she is

Ember is a Claude skill with one absolute, written into her very first paragraph: the scene gets written. Not summarized, not faded past your brand's level, not swapped for an outline or a "prompt you could use." She drafts the intimate scenes in your books — from a closed-door almost-kiss that aches, through open-door turning points, to fully explicit heat-5 dark romance — in your pen name's voice, with your reader's expectations treated as a promise to keep.

She was built after a real skill audit (the case study) showed how easily a badly-worded skill teaches your AI to flinch. Ember is worded to do the opposite.

How to summon her

EMBER… "write the heat scene where they stop pretending"
OR JUST… "write the scene where they finally—" · "make this explicit" · "give me the claiming scene" · "open the door on this" · "write the spice"
REVISION IS ONE WORD: "hotter" · "slower" · "darker" · "his POV" · "longer build"

The four facts she needs (she'll only ask for what's missing)

The emotional destination"The moment she stops bracing," "the first time he lets himself be wanted" — the line the whole scene aims at. This is the craft lever that separates competent from unforgettable.

What's baked in

  • The human heat DNA — vulnerable, dirty, fun, human. Nerves, involuntary want, real messy bodies, agency in surrender, aftermath always. The stuff that makes heat feel written by a person.
  • A full dark-romance engine — the reader contract (the reader always sees her yes; fear is thrill; obsession bends to devotion; the darkness pays a tenderness tax), plus power flips, possession language, claiming scenes, primal chase, captivity intimacy, and the escalation ladder across a book. Consenting adults, always.
  • Closed-door craft too — heat 1–2 is a lane, not an absence. She writes the hunger and closes the door on the reveal, never mid-sentence.
  • An AI-tells banned list — no tangled sheets, no slick folds, no growled/whispered tags, no "the air between us," no clock-stamp narration. Add your own pet peeves; she honors both lists.
  • Plays nice with your other skills — if you've built a pen-name or voice skill (TIP students: your Voice Skill / Pen Name Skill), it owns voice and canon; Ember brings the heat craft.

Get her installed

⬇ Download the Ember skill (.zip)

Claude (web/desktop): Settings → Capabilities → Skills → Upload skill → choose the zip.
Claude Code: unzip so the ember-heat-writer folder sits inside .claude/skills/.

First run: open references/heat-map-template.md in the skill folder and fill in your pen name row — or just answer Ember's questions the first time and ask her to save them there.
