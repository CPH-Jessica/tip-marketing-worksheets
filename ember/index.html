Get Ember for yourself

The student edition is yours to install. It's the same engine from the case study, generalized: you fill in your own pen name, heat level, and heat engine, and Ember writes at your brand — she's just as careful with a closed-door almost-kiss as she is with the scorching stuff.

⬇ Download the Ember skill (.zip) Read the skill file first

Install — two ways

  1. Claude (web/desktop): Settings → Capabilities → Skills → Upload skill → choose the zip. Done — say "Ember, write the heat scene where…" in any chat.
  2. Claude Code: unzip so the ember-heat-writer folder sits inside .claude/skills/ in your home folder or project. She's live immediately.
First run: open references/heat-map-template.md inside the skill and fill in your pen name, heat level, and heat engine — or just answer Ember's questions the first time and ask her to save them there for you.
