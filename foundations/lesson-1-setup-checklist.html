How this class works

  • Every lesson has a worksheet. The link is inside the lesson text. Open it, fill it in, and keep what you build.
  • Your answers save in the browser you use, so stick with the same computer and browser. Copy or download anything you want to keep somewhere else.
  • Go in order. Each part builds on the one before it.

The Claude Ecosystem: Quick Reference

You don't need all of this right now. It's your cheat sheet for what exists and where in the class you'll use it.

Claude (claude.ai, desktop app, phone app)

Same Claude on the website, the Mac or PC app, and your phone. Your chats follow you. Home base for the whole class.

START HERE

Instructions and memory

Where you tell Claude who you are, so you stop repeating yourself. Lives in Settings › Your Claude.

PART 2

Skills

Saved instructions for one job, like writing in your voice or beta reading a chapter. Claude uses them when the job comes up.

PART 3

Projects

A home for one book or series: your instructions and files together, so Claude opens already knowing the rules. Included with Pro.

PARTS 4 & 5

Bigger tasks and files (formerly Claude Cowork)

Since September 2026, Cowork is built into every Claude conversation. Ask for a document, spreadsheet, or research and Claude hands back the finished file. Pro can also hand off and schedule tasks.

LATER

Claude Code and Claude in Chrome

Claude Code is for developers. Claude in Chrome lets Claude work in your web browser. Both come with Pro. You don't need either for this class.
