Troubleshooting

If your "After" response isn't there yet, click the problem that matches what you're seeing.

Claude is ignoring my rules ▶

Your rules are probably buried in a wall of text, or they're phrased as suggestions instead of instructions.

Fix: Rephrase suggestions as directives. "It would be nice if you didn't use purple prose" becomes "Never use purple prose." Move rules to their own clearly labeled section with a header like "## Rules" so Claude can't miss them.

The tone is off ▶

Your relationship section is probably too vague. "Be my writing partner" is vague. Claude doesn't know what that means for you.

Fix: Add specific tone instructions. "Be direct, opinionated, and don't ask permission before giving feedback" is specific. "Don't hedge. Don't say 'you might consider.' Say 'this isn't working because.'"

Claude forgets my pen name differences ▶

Your pen names are listed without enough differentiation. Just names and genres isn't always enough.

Fix: Add heat level AND a one-line voice description for each pen name. "Sarah Westbrook — small-town romance, heat 3, warm and grounded tone. Raven Cole — dark fantasy romance, heat 5, atmospheric and intense." The more separation, the better Claude switches.

Claude is too cautious / restricted ▶

You wrote twenty rules and zero permissions. Claude is playing it safe because all it knows is what NOT to do.

Fix: Balance "never do" with "always do." Add instructions like "Always give direct feedback. Always start in the action. Always match my pen name voice. Be proactive — if you can help, say so." Give Claude room to be useful.

Response looks the same as before ▶

Two possible causes: your preferences didn't save, or you continued the old conversation instead of starting a new one.

Fix: Go to Settings → Personal Preferences and verify your text is there. Then start a completely new conversation — not the same chat window. Preferences only load at the start of a new conversation.
