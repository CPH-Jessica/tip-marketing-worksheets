2 Genre Expectations

What does a reader in your genre expect? These become the benchmarks.

One per line. What must a book in your genre deliver?
Think like a reviewer. What do the 1-star reviews in your genre complain about? Those complaints are your reader's expectations in reverse. If readers hate insta-love, your beta reader should flag when attraction moves too fast.
