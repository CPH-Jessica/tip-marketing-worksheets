LESSON 1

What a Cowork Project Actually Is

Goal: you can explain, in one sentence, what a Project is and why it beats a cold chat.

The one-sentence version

A Cowork Project is a dedicated workspace on your own computer that remembers your files, your instructions, and your context — so you never start cold again. A regular chat is a brilliant freelancer with amnesia. A Project is one trained assistant who remembers.

LESSON 3

Move Between Cowork & Chat

Goal: move your work between the two homes without losing anything.

Two homes · one folder · two doors

A Chat project lives in the cloud (browser, desktop, phone). A Cowork project does its deep work on your desktop — that's where it reaches your real files and your browser. New (July 2026): Cowork now rides along in the Claude app on your phone and the web too, so you can check on it, approve steps, and start tasks from anywhere. Your files still live locally, so the folder is the bridge and your single source of truth — back it up.

📱 New: Cowork on your phone

Why it matters for an author on the go: you start the work at your desk, then go live your life. Claude keeps running remotely; you nudge it from your phone between errands and pick up the finished draft later. Your writing time stops being chained to the chair.
