Examples by Type

Here are three examples to show you what good working-style paragraphs look like. Yours doesn't need to match these — just use them as inspiration.

INFJ — The Visionary Advisor

"I'm an INFJ. I process by thinking out loud — conversation IS my processing. I'm visionary and values-driven. I need to understand WHY before I care about HOW. When I'm brainstorming, ride the wave with me — don't try to organize it yet. When I shift to execution mode, I want clear next steps with no fluff. I make autonomous decisions and own them. I need a thinking partner who pushes back — not a yes-machine."

ISTJ — The Practical Builder

"I'm an ISTJ. Give me specifics before big picture. When I ask for help with a scene, start with the concrete problem, not the thematic implications. I like clear checklists and step-by-step processes. Don't give me abstract suggestions — tell me exactly what to change and where. I respect structure and deadlines. If I ask for a plan, give me a plan with dates."

ENFP — The Idea Generator

"I'm an ENFP. I come up with ten ideas before breakfast and need help narrowing to the best one. When I'm excited about a new direction, help me evaluate it honestly before I abandon my current project. I need someone to keep me focused without killing my energy. Start with the exciting possibility, then ground me in what's realistic."
