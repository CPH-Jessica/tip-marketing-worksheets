✅ Your KDP → Pro LLC Transition: Do This, Not That

Going legit without blowing up your accounts
The Invisible Pen · Clark Publishing House
The question: “I've formed an LLC and want to run my books like a real business — but my old KDP account and Author Central are a mess of abandoned pen names. Do I start a new KDP account? ChatGPT says Amazon's bots might flag me…”

You've already done the hard part — LLC, EIN, business bank, serious fiction mindset. Respect. The old notebook era is just digital clutter. Amazon expects legitimate business evolution; thousands do this yearly without drama when they use the official levers.

🚫 What NOT to do

  • Do NOT create a brand-new KDP account. One-account rule is ironclad. Multiple = both terminated + royalties frozen. ChatGPT is half-right on the bot risk — but only for shady duplicate accounts, not official updates.
  • Do NOT panic-merge yet. Fix first.

✅ Recommended Path (Safest + Cleanest — 95% of serious indies land here)

  1. Update your EXISTING KDP account to LLC/business (this is explicitly supported)
    • Log in → Your Account → Tax Information → Update/Review Tax Profile
    • Retake the interview → select business entity → enter LLC legal name + EIN exactly as on IRS letter.
    • Bank details: swap to new business account (expect 3-day security hold — normal).
    • Your Identity section: update to you as authorized representative (your ID must match).

    Prep docs: Articles of Organization + EIN confirmation + your driver's license. Upload if prompted. Takes 1–3 days to clear.

  2. Handle the hosed pen names separately in Author Central (this is the flexible part)
    • Author Central = different playground. One KDP can feed multiple AC accounts.
    • Option A (easiest): Log into existing AC (same creds), claim any live books under clean pen names, then email AC support: “Please clean/merge duplicate profiles under [list ASINs/old names]. All titles are mine; requesting unified clean pages for new fiction imprint.”
    • Option B (fresh start vibe): Create a brand-new AC with a different email. Claim books from your (updated) KDP. Zero penalty — many authors run 2–3 ACs for genre separation.
  3. For future books: Publisher field = your LLC or clean imprint. Author field = whatever fresh pen name. Books will auto-populate to the right AC page when you claim them.

Timeline & Next 48 Hours (do this exact sequence)

  • ✓1. Today: Open KDP → Tax Update. Screenshot any warnings so you have them handy if you need help.
  • ✓2. This week: Finish setting up the business bank account + send a polite support note to KDP if anything looks stuck.
  • ✓3. Once tax clears: Fix Author Central + test-publish one dummy book under the new pen/LLC to confirm the flow works end to end.

Click each step to check it off — your progress saves in this browser.

Real talk: Worst-case = extra verification (annoying but survivable). Best-case = you look 100% pro when your new pen names drop and your romance pens scale. No horror stories when you go through the front door like this.
